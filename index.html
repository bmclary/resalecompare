DVC Resale Comparison Tool

Enter contracts from resale broker sites to compare multiple contracts across the same or different resorts and make an informed decision on which is the best deal for your family. Once 3 or more contracts are entered select your preferred contract with the star and a recommended offer will generate. The recommended offer will price the one you want based on the best comp among the ones you've tracked. You may still want to submit an initial offer even lower but the recommended offer is where you want to land.

How this works. Total Cost sums your upfront cash (list + closing + transfer) plus projected dues through expiration, using each resort's actual historical dues CAGR, minus 2026 and 2027 dues credits on used points. Divide by Lifetime Points (unused current-year points + all future annual allotments) to get $/Point — the true cost of what you're buying, normalized for contract size, dues, and years left.
